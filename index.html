The Journey

My Story

From the outside, I was fine. I played a lot of sport, spent weekends out drinking with friends, and tried to fit the version of life I thought I was supposed to live. On the inside, I was driven by insecurity, people-pleasing and a deep fear of not being enough, and most of what I did was about making sure nobody noticed.

Even from a young age, something didn’t feel right. By university it was harder to ignore. I struggled with anxiety, isolation and low self-worth, and I was scared to really express myself. I still came out with a 2:1 in Biochemistry, but inside I felt lost.

After university, it got worse. Day after day, I couldn’t get out of bed before midday. I was too scared of failure, and of commitment, to even apply for a job. I was going nowhere, waiting for something or someone to come and save me.

In 2020, I finally saw it. I’d been drifting. I had no ambition, I didn’t think for myself, and I’d outsourced almost every part of my life. Life was something that happened to me.

Around then I started listening to the Feel Better, Live More podcast, and something landed: I had far more agency than I’d ever admitted. I started with what I could control: food, my health, my body. Better habits, more energy, and taking responsibility for my thoughts and patterns. As I rebuilt my relationship with myself, the rest followed: my career, my finances, my confidence and my sense of direction.

Then, in late 2022, I broke my ankle.

It looked like a physical injury. It turned out to be the thing that stopped me running. The existential dread I’d been outrunning my whole life caught up with me, and I had to face it, to find meaning and a purpose worth living for. That led me into years of exploring health, psychology, philosophy, spirituality and embodiment.

What I eventually realised is that no amount of outside knowledge can replace knowing yourself. You can chase information, success and approval forever, but if your relationship with yourself is built on fear or unworthiness, it will keep shaping your life. The real work wasn’t fixing myself. It was reconnecting with who I was underneath the conditioning and the fear.

Today, I take full ownership of my health and my life. This work is built around one thing: helping you take ownership of yours, and use your gifts to build a life, and a living, that’s truly your own.

If any of this sounds familiar (the drifting, the waiting to be saved, the quiet sense that you’re capable of more), that’s where I started too. It’s also where the work begins.

The Process

What to Expect

Simple structure. Real life. Your pace.

There's no script and no set curriculum here. What we have is a framework, and we return to it as life unfolds. You won't be judged or rushed, and you won't be handed a plan that doesn't fit you. Here's the shape of the work, and what a typical week looks like.

Your Journey

Know Yourself

1

Self-awareness

Notice the patterns, beliefs and protective habits quietly steering your life, and meet them with curiosity instead of criticism.

Lead Yourself

2

Clarity

Work out what matters to you and what you truly want, separate from what you were taught to want.

3

Commitment

Decide who you want to be and what you'll stand behind, and renew that choice each week.

Create Your Life

4

Action

Take small, honest steps in your everyday life, and keep taking them.

5

Reflection

Look back at what happened, what got in the way and what it taught you, then carry that forward. This is where growth compounds.

Life doesn't move in a straight line, and neither will we. We'll circle back to any step whenever you need to.
